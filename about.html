About Akib

I study electrical and electronic engineering and enjoy learning by building things with a team.

A little context

I am finishing a degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at AIUB and expect to graduate in 2027. Lately I have been spending time on digital design and embedded systems while building my skills in VLSI, SystemVerilog, C/C++ and MATLAB.

One of my main projects is a four-person capstone on an autonomous wheelchair for safer navigation and remote biomedical monitoring. Our team brought machine learning together with Raspberry Pi 5 and ESP32-based hardware. I also worked with a six-person group on a smart farm project built around the ATmega328P.

I enjoy working with a team and finding a clear way to explain technical ideas. Presenting our capstone to supervisors and external evaluators helped me grow more comfortable talking through engineering work. I am now looking for an internship where I can keep learning by doing.
