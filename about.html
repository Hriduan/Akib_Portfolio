A little context

I am a final-year Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at AIUB, expected to graduate in 2027. I enjoy digital design and embedded systems and I am building my skills in VLSI, SystemVerilog, C/C++ and MATLAB.

My capstone project, “An Autonomous Wheelchair for Safe Navigation and Remote Biomedical Monitoring,” brings machine learning together with Raspberry Pi 5 and ESP32-based embedded hardware. I have also worked with a six-member team on an ATmega328P-based smart farm system.

I value clear communication and collaborative engineering. I have presented technical work to supervisors and external evaluators and I am keen to keep learning through hands-on projects and an engineering internship.
